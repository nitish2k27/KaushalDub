Which one to use

Live demo

Hybrid

Whisper on the server, Gemini free tier, Sarvam for translation and voice. Takes about 2 minutes a video with natural Bulbul voices, at about ₹7 a video. The ₹100 sign-up credit covers the four demo videos several times over.

Bulk evaluation

All local on Kaggle

IndicTrans2 and Indic Parler-TTS on the free T4 cost ₹0, but take 5–8 minutes a video. The 30-video playlist needs about 3–4 GPU hours of the 30-hour weekly quota. Use this for the report's provider comparison.

Only if needed

All APIs

Sarvam speech-to-text adds about ₹0.5 a minute. It helps for Hindi or Kannada speech, where Whisper-small is weaker. For English instructors, Whisper is free and accurate enough.

Out of credits: Sarvam returns HTTP 402 when the prepaid balance is zero and never auto-charges unless auto-recharge is on. KaushalDub then falls back to the local model for that stage, or pauses the job as "needs credits" with a Resume button. Gemini and Groq free tiers return 429 when exhausted; the app retries, then falls back to Groq and finally to template text.

Sources and assumptions

  • Sarvam pay-as-you-go, from sarvam.ai/api-pricing: speech to text ₹0.49/min, translate ₹2.00 per 1,000 characters, Bulbul v3 voice ₹3.00 per 1,000 characters, ₹100 free credit. GST extra.
  • Narration length follows build_prompt: at most 2 words per second of step. The LLM usually fills about 70% of that, at about 5.8 characters per English word; Hindi and Kannada text is taken as 1.1× longer. Translation is billed on the English input, and the quiz adds about 350 characters per language.
  • Gemini and Groq free tiers cost ₹0 within their rate limits. A paid Gemini Flash call for one video is far below ₹1.
  • Local timing on a Kaggle T4: Whisper-small about 15 s, step model about 10 s, IndicTrans2 about 30 s (first run also installs its environment, 3–5 min), Indic Parler-TTS about 20 s per step clip (the dub-check notebook measured 24 s). Kaggle gives 30 free GPU hours a week.
