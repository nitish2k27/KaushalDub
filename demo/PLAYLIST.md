# KaushalDub demo playlist

30 YouTube videos from the **COIN test split** (never used to train the step model), 5 per skill, at least 5 per
domain, all checked online on 2026-11-07. `demo/playlist.csv` has the same list with COIN ground-truth steps;
`demo/ground_truth.json` is used by `backend/scripts/demo_check.py`.

**Live demo videos** (downloaded to `demo/videos/`, marked ★): one per domain, 80–100 s, with the most annotated steps.
Upload the files during the demo instead of pasting links, so a slow network or a YouTube block cannot break it.
